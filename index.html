01 — FULL VIEW

One camera.
No blind spots.

The 4K Omni uses a 360° rotating design powered by dual Hall Effect sensors and a magnetic system.

The result is precise lens positioning and smooth rotation, allowing the camera to monitor different directions around your vehicle.

02 — TRUE 4K

Details that stay visible.

Sony IMX678 technology combined with a 12nm quad-core processor delivers high-resolution imaging and detailed footage.

4K at 60 FPS is available in single-channel mode, while dual-channel recording supports up to 4K at 30 FPS.
